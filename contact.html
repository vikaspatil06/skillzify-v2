Frequently asked questions

Quick answers — no email needed.

Is Skillzify really free?

Yes — every lesson, exercise, project and reference entry is free, with no paywalls or premium tier.

Do I need an account?

No. You can learn entirely without one. Signing in (free) syncs your progress across devices.

Where is my progress stored?

In your browser's local storage. If you sign in, it also syncs to your Firebase-backed account so your phone and laptop stay in step.

The compiler won't load. What do I do?

It downloads the Python runtime (~10 MB) on first use, so the very first load needs a stable connection. If it still fails, reload the page — and if that doesn't help, send us a bug report with your browser name.

Found a mistake in a lesson or exercise?

Thank you! Use the form above with the Bug report topic (or open a GitHub Issue) and tell us the page name and what's wrong.

Can I request a lesson, exercise or project?

Absolutely — that's what the Content request topic is for. Tell us what you want to learn and we'll consider it for the roadmap.
