Our story

Skillzify started as a simple Python learning site and grew into a browser-first learning platform: structured lessons, hands-on practice with an AI tutor, guided learning paths, real-world projects, a searchable Python reference, a real Python runtime in the browser, and analytics that show exactly where you're improving.

No installs. No account required to start. Just open the site and write your first line of Python.

What we believe

Three principles behind every page on this site.

🆓

Free forever

Every lesson, exercise, project and reference entry is free. No paywalls, no premium tier, no catch.

🌐

Browser-first

No installs, no setup. The compiler, lessons and UI run entirely in your browser.*

🔒

Your data stays yours

Progress lives in your browser — and syncs through your own account when you sign in. No ads, no selling your data — anonymous usage analytics only.

*The compiler downloads the Python runtime (~10 MB) on first use.
