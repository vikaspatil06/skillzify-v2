Questions, answered

Is Skillzify really free?

Yes — lessons, exercises, the compiler, Flowchart Lab and the community are all free, and you can start without an account. AI answers are capped per day (5 a day anonymously, 10 a day signed in) so the shared AI budget stays fair for everyone.

Do I need to install Python?

No. Python 3.11 runs entirely in your browser (via Pyodide), in lessons, exercises and Code Studio — including input(), files and popular packages like NumPy and pandas. You only need an internet connection the first time the runtime loads.

How does unlocking work?

Lessons unlock in order: complete one to open the next, across all 7 sections. If you already know Python, the placement test can place you further along and unlock everything before your starting point — without marking anything as done.

Do I get certificates?

Yes. Finishing a section earns a downloadable certificate, and your Skill Passport gathers them with your level, XP and achievements into one shareable record of progress.

Does my progress sync across devices?

Yes. Create a free account and your lessons, XP, streak, exercises, reviews, workspace and passport sync through Firebase — start on your laptop, continue on your phone, pick up exactly where you left off.

Is there an Android app?

Yes — a native Android app mirrors the website: the same 250 lessons, 150 exercises, compiler, Flowchart Lab and AI, signed in with the same account so progress stays in sync.
