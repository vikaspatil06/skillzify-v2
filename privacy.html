Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Skillzify is a free Python learning platform. This page explains, in plain language, what data the site handles, why, and the choices you have. Questions: skillzifyind2024@gmail.com.

What we collect

Account data. If you create an account, we store your email address and display name through Firebase Authentication so you can sign in.

Learning progress. Lessons completed, XP, streaks, exercise results, achievements and the code you save in Code Studio are stored in your browser. If you sign in, they also sync to your account in our database (Google Firestore) so your progress follows you across devices.

Community content. Questions, answers and showcase projects you post in the Skillzify Circle are public, as the community pages explain.

Interview practice. If you upload a resume for AI Interview practice, its text is extracted in your browser and used only to tailor your practice session. Sharing a result with an institution is always your choice, off by default, and shares an anonymised summary only.

AI features

When you ask the AI tutor a question or request an explanation, your question and the relevant code are sent to our AI service providers to generate the answer. Please don't paste personal information — passwords, phone numbers, private keys — into code, questions or interview answers.

Cookies, analytics and advertising

Essential storage. The site uses your browser's local storage for your theme, progress and sign-in session. Clearing your browser data removes it.

Analytics. We use Google Analytics to understand anonymous, aggregate usage — which pages are visited and which features are used — so we can improve the platform. We do not sell personal data.

Advertising. Skillzify is free for learners and is supported in part by advertising served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can manage or turn off personalised ads in Google Ads Settings, and learn how Google uses data from sites that use its services at policies.google.com.

Your choices

You can use Skillzify without an account — progress simply stays in your browser. You can clear site data at any time from your browser settings. To have your account and its synced data deleted, email us at skillzifyind2024@gmail.com and we will remove it.

Younger learners

Skillzify is built for students and self-learners. If you are under 18, please use it with the guidance of a parent, guardian or teacher, especially before creating an account or posting in the community.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we will update the date at the top of this page. Continuing to use Skillzify after a change means you accept the updated policy.
